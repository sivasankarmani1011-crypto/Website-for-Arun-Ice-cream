About Arun Ice Cream

For over 15 years, Arun Ice Cream has been a beloved landmark of joy in Kallal. What began as a cozy corner shop has blossomed into the community's favorite spot for cool, refreshing treats.

We believe in quality ingredients, welcoming smiles, and affordable happiness for every single customer who walks through our doors.
